kotdoc mathematical Unicode/TeX mapping translation (2026)

This modified Kotlin work is named MathTeXUnicode.kt and
MathTeXUnicodeData.kt in package io.heapy.kotdoc.math. It is maintained as part
of https://github.com/Heapy/kotdoc; support for this translation belongs there.

It translates Text.TeXMath.Unicode.ToTeX from texmath 0.13.3.1, copyright
2014 Matthew Pickering, distributed under GPL version 2 or later. That module
derives from the Unicode/LaTeX mapping copyright 2011 Günter Milde, originally
distributed under LPPL version 1.3 or later. The original module's additional
LPPL clause-6 notice is retained in its complete unmodified source.

Changes: Kotlin types and immutable collections replace Haskell types; the
ordered record table is generated as Kotlin source; lookup, package choice,
symbol-map and Unicode fallback algorithms are translated to Kotlin. This is
a distinct renamed work, not an update published by the upstream authors.

The unmodified upstream module and original mapping are retained in:
vendor/texmath-0.13.3.1/src/Text/TeXMath/Unicode/ToTeX.hs
vendor/texmath-0.13.3.1/lib/totexmath/unimathsymbols.txt

Their complete source release is available at:
https://hackage.haskell.org/package/texmath-0.13.3.1/texmath-0.13.3.1.tar.gz

The original GPL and LPPL texts accompany this work as Texmath-GPL-2.0.txt
and LPPL-1.3c.txt. The unmodified originals remain available independently
of this translation; names, credits and notices have not been removed.
